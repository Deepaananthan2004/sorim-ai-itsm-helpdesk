# VPN Troubleshooting
approval_status: approved

Symptoms: VPN cannot connect, authentication fails, or the connection repeatedly disconnects.

Steps:
1. Confirm the device has a working internet connection.
2. Verify the organization-approved VPN profile and gateway.
3. Check the device date and time.
4. Complete the approved multi-factor authentication prompt.
5. Disconnect and reconnect once; do not repeatedly retry if the account may be locked.
6. Record the exact error and time if the issue continues.

Escalation: Network Support should investigate persistent authentication failures or an unavailable gateway. Never share passwords or MFA codes in tickets.

# Microsoft Teams Troubleshooting
approval_status: approved

Steps:
1. Confirm network connectivity and check the IT-approved service status page.
2. Verify the correct work account.
3. Quit and reopen Teams normally.
4. Check microphone and camera permissions if calls are affected.
5. Record the error and whether chat, meetings, or sign-in is affected.

Do not delete managed profiles or disable security software without IT guidance. Contact the service desk if failures persist.
